# Audify Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-30

Audify plays a short sound when you press a button, hover over a button, or click a link on a
web page. It does this entirely inside your browser.

**What Audify reads on web pages.** On each page, Audify notices when you press, hover over, or
activate (with Enter or Space) a button or link, so it knows when to play a sound. It does not read,
store, or send the text, contents, address, or any other information from the pages you visit.

**What Audify stores.** Only your Audify settings: whether sounds are on, which sound plays for
each action, the volume, the pitch-variation option, and the list of sites you have muted (by
domain name, such as `example.com`). These are kept in your browser's extension storage. If you
are signed in to your browser with sync turned on, your browser syncs these settings to your other
devices, the same way it syncs other extension settings. Audify itself has no server.

**What Audify sends.** Nothing. Audify makes no network requests, uses no analytics or tracking,
and shares no data with anyone. All sounds are generated on your device.

**Removing your data.** Uninstalling Audify removes its settings from your browser.
